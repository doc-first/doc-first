A01 What Doc First demands of a page

This page is the smallest working example. If it works in your Doc First, your documentation will too.

1 The rules a page has to follow

Everything that can be approved lives inside <main>. What sits outside is not reviewable — not the header, not the footer, not the menu.

Approval covers the text, not the block. Doc First keeps a fingerprint of the text taken at the moment of the ✓. Change one letter and the approval stops counting — on purpose, because nobody approved the new text.

Hence the rule that is easiest to forget: anything JavaScript injects inside <main> needs the data-revisao-ui attribute. Without it, the injected text counts toward the fingerprint and drops every approval on the page at once.

2 What happens when you approve

Nothing is erased. Every ✓, every request and every refusal becomes a new event, with author and time. The database refuses updates and deletes — not out of discipline, by trigger.

One person approves: the owner in doc-first.json. An approval from anyone else is recorded, but never becomes a lock in the repository.
