T01 One screen, explained

One sheet per screen. The prototype can be HTML, React, Figma or a sketch — what this sheet holds is what the screen does, which survives any change of tool.

1 What the person came here to do

One sentence. If it takes two, it is two screens.

Say the end, not the means: "check whether the payment can be released", not "see the list".

2 What they see

The elements, and where each piece of data comes from.

List what appears and the origin of every piece of information. A field with no declared origin becomes, later, a question for the backend on the eve of delivery.

3 What they can do

The actions, each with what happens next.

One line per action: what the person clicks, what the system does, where they land.

⚠️ Include the ugly states: empty, loading, error, not allowed. The happy screen is the least work; the other four are what set the deadline.

4 The prototype

The link, and which parts of it are fake.

Point at the prototype and say, explicitly, what is mocked. A prototype without that note always turns into a promise somebody comes to collect.
