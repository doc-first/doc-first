I01 What comes in and what goes out

The edges of the system: APIs, events, files. A contract is a promise — and a promise broken here breaks somebody else's system, which is the worst way to find out.

1 The routes

Method, path, what it takes and what it returns.

For each one: what it does, who may call it, and the error codes with the reason. "400 when the body is invalid" does not help; "400 when the tax ID is missing" does.

2 The events

What the system publishes, and who depends on it.

Name, when it fires, and what it carries. And — the field everybody forgets — whether it can arrive twice.

3 What does not change without warning

The part of the contract that is a public promise.

List what other systems already depend on. Changing that takes a new version, not a deploy. ⚠️ If the list is empty, either the system is new or somebody did not ask.
