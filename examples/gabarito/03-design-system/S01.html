S01 What the product looks like, and why

This sheet exists so the tenth screen looks like the first. It is about decisions on appearance, not a catalogue of components.

1 The values

Colour, type, spacing and radius — the numbers, not the names.

List the values with the number alongside: the colour in hex, the size in rem. "Primary blue" is not a value; "#0883C5" is.

⚠️ Contrast: give the measured ratio of every text colour against the background it sits on. Below 4.5:1 in body text does not pass, and it is better to find that out here than in the audit.

2 The components

What exists, and when to reach for each one.

One paragraph per component, saying when to use it — and when not to. A catalogue that only shows the looks stops nobody from choosing wrong.

3 What is not done here

The list of "no", which is the most useful of them all.

No modal inside a modal. No text below 14px. No colour as the only carrier of meaning. Write your own. This list saves more review than any other.
