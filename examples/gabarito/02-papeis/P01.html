P01 Who can do what

A role is not a job title: it is a set of capabilities. Write it in terms of what the person can do, not what they are called on the org chart.

1 The roles

Few of them, with a clear border.

One paragraph per role: what it can do that the others cannot. If two roles have the same list, they are one role.

⚠️ Every product ends up with a role that "can do everything". Name it here, and say how many people hold it. If the answer is "everyone on the team", you do not have roles.

2 The capability table

Who can read, create, approve and delete each thing.

One row per object in the system, one column per role. It is dull to write and it is the sheet that spares the most argument later.

Mark what nobody can do: delete an audit record, edit someone else's approval. What nobody can do is a rule, and a rule becomes code.

3 Who approves

The decision that is not delegated.

Say who has the final word on each kind of content, by role name. An approval with no named owner is an approval that never happens.
